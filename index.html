I am a Ph.D. candidate in Operations Research at Cornell University, advised by Omar El Housni. My research is on combinatorial optimization and decision making under uncertainty, with applications to revenue management and online platforms. Before Cornell, I received an M.Sc. in Finance and a B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from Sharif University of Technology.
